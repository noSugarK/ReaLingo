模型与价格Models & pricing

ReaLingo 免费，翻译用你自己的 API Key，按厂商价格计费。ReaLingo is free. Translation uses your own API key, billed at the vendor's rates.

价格为 查询的官方价目（不含促销），以控制台为准。每分钟价按官方音频 token 单价估算（Qwen 输入每秒 7 个、输出 12.5 个；豆包输入 6.25 个、输出 25 个），OpenAI 和 Gemini 取官方每分钟价；译文文本另计，通常远小于音频部分。免费额度的数量、期限和开通方式常有变动，以各家控制台实际显示为准。Official list prices checked , promotions excluded; the console has the final word. Per-minute figures convert the audio token rates (Qwen 7 tokens/s in, 12.5 out; Doubao 6.25 in, 25 out); OpenAI and Gemini publish per-minute prices. Translated text is billed on top and is usually far smaller than the audio. Free quotas change often in amount, duration and how to activate them; what the vendor console shows is what applies.
